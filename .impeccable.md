## Design Context

### Users

South Florida homeowners, facility managers, and business owners evaluating a durable epoxy coating for a garage, interior, commercial, industrial, patio, or countertop project. They arrive on a phone or desktop to understand the available systems, judge finished quality, and quickly request a credible estimate.

### Brand Personality

Precise, capable, and premium. The experience should communicate technical craft and polished results without feeling cold, flashy, or like a generic home-services template.

### Aesthetic Direction

Dark metallic surfaces, controlled gloss, silver details, and restrained electric cobalt accents. Mobile should feel deliberately thumb-friendly inside a browser; larger screens should use spacious, editorial composition with enterprise-grade alignment and hierarchy.

### Design Principles

1. Put the finish, proof, and next conversion action in immediate reach.
2. Use contrast, spacing, and real material imagery to create confidence rather than ornamental effects.
3. Preserve a consistent information architecture across phone, tablet, and desktop while adapting controls for touch and keyboard use.
4. Treat motion as feedback and orientation, with a complete reduced-motion experience.
5. Make every interactive state accessible, reversible, and resilient to client-side navigation.
