PRVN Epoxy Solutions Inc. ("PRVN Epoxy Solutions," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when you visit our website, submit a form (including forms on Facebook or Instagram), request an estimate, contact our team, or otherwise interact with our business.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

- First and last name

- Email address

- Phone number

- Property address or service location

- Information about your flooring or epoxy project

- Any other information you submit through our website forms, Facebook or Instagram lead forms, phone calls, text messages, or emails

We may also automatically collect certain technical information when you visit our website, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, referring website, and general website usage information.

PRVN Epoxy Solutions Inc. may use the information we collect to:

- Respond to inquiries and requests for estimates

- Contact you about your flooring, coating, or related project

- Provide, schedule, and coordinate requested services

- Prepare estimates and project information

- Send appointment reminders and service updates

- Send promotional messages, only where you have separately consented to receive them

- Respond to questions, comments, or requests

- Improve our website, services, and customer experience

- Maintain business and customer records

- Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

- Protect our business, website, customers, and users from fraudulent or unauthorized activity

## 3. Forms and Communications

If you submit your name, email address, phone number, or project information through a PRVN Epoxy Solutions Inc. website form or a Facebook or Instagram lead form, we may use that information to respond to your request.

## 4. SMS / Text Messaging

If you provide your mobile phone number and check the box consenting to receive text messages, PRVN Epoxy Solutions Inc. may send you text messages related to your inquiry, estimates, appointment scheduling, and service updates. If you separately consent to marketing messages, we may also send promotional offers.

- Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

- Message frequency varies.

- Message and data rates may apply.

- You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. After you reply STOP, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further messages unless you opt in again.

- Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at [(954) 388-7112](tel:+19543887112) or [prvnepoxy@outlook.com](mailto:prvnepoxy@outlook.com).

- Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

## 5. Information Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We may share personal information only when reasonably necessary to:

- Provide requested services

- Communicate with you about your project

- Work with service providers that support our business operations (such as website hosting, CRM, scheduling, and communication platforms), who may only use the information to provide services to us

- Comply with legal obligations or lawful requests

- Protect the rights, property, safety, and security of PRVN Epoxy Solutions Inc., our customers, or others
